## Sample CBIC Circular 55/2025: Input Tax Credit and E-Invoicing

The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) clarifies Rule 36Z of the Sample CGST Rules. Input Tax Credit (ITC) may be availed only on invoices that appear in the recipient's auto-populated statement GSTR-2B. Rule 36Z applies in addition to the conditions in Section 16 of the Sample CGST Act.

Earlier, ITC under Rule 36Z was allowed up to 105 percent of the credit reflected in GSTR-2B for a tax period. The 57th GST Council withdrew this tolerance, so ITC is now restricted to 100 percent of the credit reflected in GSTR-2B. The Council also recommended that e-invoicing be mandatory for taxpayers with annual aggregate turnover above Rs 5 crore.

Where a supplier fails to file the GSTR-1 return, the invoice does not reflect in GSTR-2B and the recipient cannot claim ITC for that invoice until the supplier files. A recipient who claims ITC in violation of Rule 36Z must reverse the credit with interest under Section 50 and may face penalty under Section 122.

Input Service Distributor (ISD) credit must be distributed in the same month as the ISD invoice is issued, as required by the ISD rules, and cannot be distributed to units not eligible for ITC.
