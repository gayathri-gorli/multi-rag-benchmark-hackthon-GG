## Sample CBDT Circular 101/2025: Concessional Regime for Domestic Companies

The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) issues this circular to clarify the operation of Section 115ZA of the Sample Income Tax Act. Section 115ZA allows a domestic company to opt for a concessional tax rate of 22 percent, provided the company does not claim specified deductions and exemptions.

Clarification 1: The option under Section 115ZA, once exercised, is irrevocable for the assessment year and all subsequent years. This clarification is retrospective and applies from the assessment year 2020-21.

Clarification 2: A company that opts for Section 115ZA is not eligible to claim Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT) credit under Section 115ZB. Unabsorbed MAT credit lapses on exercise of the option.

Clarification 3: The amendment inserting the 22 percent rate is prospective and applies only from the assessment year 2020-21 onwards. Tax Authority officers shall not reopen assessments for earlier years on this ground.

Form 10-ZA must be filed electronically before the due date of the return of income to exercise the option. Failure to file Form 10-ZA attracts a late filing fee under Section 234ZF.
