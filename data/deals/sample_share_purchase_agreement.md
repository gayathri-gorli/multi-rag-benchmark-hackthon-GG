## Sample Share Purchase Agreement: Orion Buyer Pvt Ltd and Vega Holdings Ltd

Clause 8.1 (Indemnity): The Seller, Vega Holdings Ltd, shall indemnify the Buyer, Orion Buyer Pvt Ltd, against losses arising from breach of the warranties in Clause 7. The Seller's aggregate liability under Clause 8.1 is capped at Rs 40 crore, being 20 percent of the Purchase Price of Rs 200 crore.

Clause 8.3 (Basket): No claim may be made under Clause 8.1 until aggregate losses exceed Rs 1 crore (the Basket). Once exceeded, the Buyer may claim from the first rupee.

Clause 9.2 (Material Adverse Change): The Buyer may terminate before Closing if a Material Adverse Change (MAC) occurs. A MAC means an event that reduces consolidated EBITDA of the target by more than 15 percent against the financial year 2024-25 baseline.

Clause 11 (Foreign Investment): The Buyer is a non-resident controlled entity. The investment is subject to the FEMA foreign direct investment pricing guidelines, and the Purchase Price must not be lower than the fair value determined by a registered valuer under Section 247 of the Companies Act, 2013.
