## Sample Enterprise Risk Framework Aligned to ISO 31000 and SEBI BRSR

Clause 4.2 of this framework adopts the ISO 31000 risk management process: establish context, identify risks, analyse, evaluate and treat. The Chief Risk Officer owns the enterprise risk register and reports quarterly to the Audit Committee.

Control C-17 (Customer Due Diligence Control) maps to RBI KYC Paragraph 12 and Paragraph 18. Control C-18 (Suspicious Transaction Reporting Control) maps to RBI KYC Paragraph 24 and the PMLA reporting obligation to FIU-IND. Each control has a named owner and is tested by Internal Audit once a year.

SEBI's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) guidelines require listed entities to disclose governance and risk management policies. Core indicator P1-E3 requires disclosure of the number of complaints on ethics and anti-corruption. Failure to file BRSR on time attracts a penalty under the SEBI listing regulations.

Where a control test fails, the failure is rated by severity. A Severity 1 failure involving a PMLA obligation must be escalated to the Chief Risk Officer within 24 hours.
