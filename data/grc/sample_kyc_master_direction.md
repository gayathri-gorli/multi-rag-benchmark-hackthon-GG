## Sample RBI Master Direction on KYC (Illustrative)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) requires every regulated entity to implement a Customer Due Diligence (CDD) programme under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA). Paragraph 12 requires identity verification before an account relationship is established.

Paragraph 18: Customers classified as high risk must undergo enhanced due diligence and periodic KYC updation at least every 2 years. Medium risk customers are updated every 8 years and low risk customers every 10 years.

Paragraph 24: A regulated entity must report suspicious transactions to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) within 7 working days of arriving at the conclusion that a transaction is suspicious. Cash transactions above Rs 10 lakh in a month must be reported by the 15th of the following month.

Penalty: Non-compliance with Paragraph 18 or Paragraph 24 attracts a monetary penalty of up to Rs 1 crore per instance under the PMLA enforcement framework, and repeated breaches may lead to restriction of business. The internal audit function must test these controls annually and report gaps to the Audit Committee.
